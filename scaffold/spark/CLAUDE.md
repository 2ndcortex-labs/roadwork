The spark sub-register — ideas memorialized for later consideration. One
subfolder per spark, kebab-case item name, never reused, holding exactly one file:
`idea.md` — the Operator's notes verbatim, the filer's reading, and the answers
of one round of mini-intentify folded in. No frontmatter, no intent, plan, or
spec; nothing blocks on a spark and it has no place in `ordering.md`. A spark
is never worked. Its one exit is conversion: when an idea becomes a todo or
feature (filed through `/roadwork-ingest` with the `idea.md` as its notes), the spark
moves whole into `archive/` on the Operator's word. Entered through
`/roadwork-spark`.
