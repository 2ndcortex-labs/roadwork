Guidebooks — one `<guidebook-slug>.md` per guidebook: a building block for architecture, a standardized knowledge base, in the fixed form `/roadwork-guidebook` writes. It opens with a header block of three keys, always present: `kind`, `atlas`, `destination`. Kind `atlas` names one atlas, kind `destination` one destination, the other key empty; kind `template` is never kept here, but in `outgoing/`. Any number may name one atlas or one destination. A guidebook with no header block is unscoped, made earlier: listed and drawn on as before, reaching nothing by scope. The guidebooks that reach a destination or a work item are computed from header blocks by the skill's `guidebooks.mjs reach`; a map copies their relevant parts in. A retired guidebook moves to `archive/` by hand; a destination's guidebook stays when its destination is archived.
A document received in chat or by path, or a template copied from the organization's store, is saved beside its guidebook as `<guidebook-slug>.source.md`, verbatim. Road plans law: the register rule (the Guidebooks bullet).
The form, in this order: the header block; `# Guidebook: <title in the Operator's words>`; `_Guidebook, made <date> by /roadwork-guidebook; open until retired._`; the `**Architectures:**` line; what it covers; then `## Standards`, `## Stack` (where it carries a stack), `## Vendors`, `## Judgements`, the item parts chosen in the interview (`## Hosts`, `## MCPs`, `## Coding standards: <language>`, `## SOPs`, `## Best practices`, or a name the Operator gives), `## Sources`, `## Changelog`. An unscoped guidebook made earlier opens at its `# Guidebook:` line.
