# Ordering

One ordered list; top is next. A line is `- <ref> — <why it sits here>`; a
group is an indented block under one line and moves as a whole. Archived items
are struck at archiving. Loose by design: an order, not a schedule — no dates,
no estimates, no claim of completeness. Maintained by agent judgment; reviewed
by the Operator.
References: same-home `todo/<item-name>` `feature/<item-name>`; from anywhere
`main/<kind>/<item-name>`, or the alias your project's law gives the prefix.
